Background Remover

You upload any image, I remove the background — an AI model runs the whole thing right here in your browser, nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Runs a real segmentation model client-side (via @imgly/background-removal). The first run downloads about 40MB and caches it in your browser, so later uses are much faster. Every new image still needs a fresh pass through the model though — that part can't be skipped, it's genuinely doing the work each time.
