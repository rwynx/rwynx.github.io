Background Remover

You upload any image, I remove the background — an AI model runs the whole thing right here in your browser, your image is never sent to any server.

Runs a real segmentation model client-side (via @imgly/background-removal). If you're running it for the first time, it downloads a ~40MB model, so depending on your connection it can take a few seconds to a minute. Each new image needs its own fresh pass through the model — that part can't be skipped, it's genuinely doing the work every time. Drag the handle on the preview to compare before/after.
